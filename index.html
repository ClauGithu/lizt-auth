Frequently asked questions

Is the free generation really free?

Yes. Every new account gets one complete property generation — including PDF, Instagram post, Story, Carousel, email, and video. No credit card required to start.

What is Auto Social Post?

Auto Social Post is a PRO feature that lets you connect your Instagram account and publish directly from Lizt with one click — no copy-paste, no switching apps.

What is Quantiq AI?

Quantiq AI is the platform that powers Lizt. When you sign up, you create a Quantiq AI account that gives you access to Lizt and other tools built on the platform.

Do I need design or video editing skills?

No. Everything is generated automatically. Just fill in the property details and upload photos.

How long does it take to generate a full listing?

Between 2 and 5 minutes for all materials: PDF, images, video, and email.

How do credits work?

Each asset you generate uses credits: a PDF listing, a post, or an email costs 1 credit; a carousel costs 2; AI voice costs 3; a full Video Reel costs 10 credits. Credits renew every month with your subscription.

Does it work for any property type?

Yes. Houses, apartments, commercial spaces, land, offices, and more.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes, no penalties or contracts. Cancel from your account whenever you want.

What languages can I generate content in?

Spanish and English. You choose the language for each listing you generate.
